How our rankings work

Standings, weekly awards, head-to-head and the monthly and quarterly prizes each use a different rule. Pick a topic and try the examples. All the names and numbers in the examples are made up.

The main table

The table is ordered by total FPL points, the same total you see in the FPL app. The GW Pts column is your score for the latest gameweek.

If you made extra transfers, FPL takes 4 points off for each one. That hit is already inside your total, and it is already taken off your GW score. A red tag next to a score shows the hit that was taken.

Awards handed out every gameweek

Manager of the Week is the highest score after hits. Donkey of the Week is the lowest score after hits. If two teams tie, they share the award.

That is why a team can score the most raw points and still miss out. The hit counts against it.

Head-to-head

Each gameweek you play one team in the league. The higher FPL score wins. Hits do not count here. Both teams are compared on the points they scored before any deduction.

A win is 3 points, a draw is 1 and a loss is 0.

How the H2H table is ordered

First by H2H points. If teams are level, the better difference goes higher. Difference is all the FPL points a team scored in its matches minus all the points scored against it.

Manager of the Month

Add up each team's score after hits across every gameweek in the month. The highest total wins. The award is handed out automatically once the month's last gameweek is over and FPL has confirmed its bonus points.

Tap a month to see its gameweeks

Quarterly awards

The season is split into four quarters. In each one, three awards go to the teams whose players earned the most points in one part of the pitch. Only players in your starting XI count, including captain bonuses and auto-subs.

Quarterly winners are locked in automatically after the quarter's last gameweek. Until then the Awards page shows running totals.

Quick answers

Why is my gameweek score lower than what my players got?

Your hit comes off. A -4 tag next to your score on the Standings page shows it.

Why did someone with fewer points win Manager of the Week?

Awards use the score after hits. A team that scored more but took a hit can finish below a team that made no extra transfers.

Do hits matter in head-to-head?

No. Head-to-head compares the points scored before any hit.

When are the monthly and quarterly awards decided?

Automatically, once the last gameweek of the month or quarter is over and FPL has confirmed the bonus points. That can be a few hours after the last match.

What happens if two teams tie?

They share the award. In the head-to-head table, ties on points are split by difference.

What if someone is suspended from the league?

They drop out of the standings and leaderboards from that gameweek onward. Their earlier gameweeks stay on record.
